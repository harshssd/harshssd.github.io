Coupang · Staff Software Engineer · Fulfillment Tech · 2025 —

Many fulfillment centers. One box at your door.

I architected and shipped Cross-FC Consolidation (CFCC) from scratch: a logistics routing engine that breaks an order into shipments, then brings items from several fulfillment centers back together into a single parcel.

Underneath it, I moved complex workflows off Kafka choreography onto Temporal — nested orchestration across four core systems, five state machines, twenty-plus status codes and sub-second compensation — designed so eight engineers could build in parallel against one shared spec.

Simplify before you scale

The best architecture change is often a removal: fewer network hops, fewer moving parts, fewer pages at 3 a.m.

  • Kafka choreography → Temporal orchestration
  • Federated GraphQL → targeted services
  • Fragmented localization → one platform
