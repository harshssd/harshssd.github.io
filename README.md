# harshssd.github.io

Personal site of Harsha Hassan Somashekara.

Concept: **Depth of Field**: the page is viewed through a camera viewfinder. An aperture opens on load, and whatever sits on the focal plane (the middle of the screen) is sharp while the rest falls out of focus. Tap the `f/1.4` button to stop down to `f/16` and make everything sharp.

One hand-written `index.html`, no frameworks or build step. Project screenshots live in `assets/shots/`.
